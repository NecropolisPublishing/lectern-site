Become a Supporter

Lectern is free for everyone, for any purpose — including your professional work. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks the deeper writing tools. The Supporter tier is optional: if Lectern benefits your organization, it’s a way to help keep the work independent and user-supported.

AI assistance is optional and uses your own API key — you connect your OpenRouter account and pay that provider directly, and Voice works the same way with your own Deepgram key. Lectern never hosts or generates content itself, and never touches your key beyond your own device.
